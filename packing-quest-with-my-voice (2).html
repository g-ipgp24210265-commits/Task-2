👩‍🏫 Teacher’s Guide (click to open)

Aim

Pupils practise listening for specific information, solving everyday trip problems with reasons, and speaking in a short voice message, using the language of packing and going away.

How to use (about 30 minutes)

  1. Stage 1 (10 min): pupils listen, use the script only if needed, and answer the 3 questions.
  2. Stage 2 (10 min): pupils read the visitor notice, choose the best item for each problem and say why.
  3. Stage 3 (10 min): pupils choose who the message is for, build their sentences, record, listen back, tick the self-check and save the message.

Support and extension

  • Support: show the script, replay the audio, use the sentence frames and read the notice aloud.
  • Extension: pupils change the items and reasons in the frames, or record a second pitch for a different destination.

Assessment ideas

  • Quiz and challenge results show listening and reading for information.
  • Pupils’ saved voice messages can be used for speaking feedback, using the four self-check points.

Note on the visitor notice

The notice is a short text written for this lesson. You may replace it with a real, simplified visitor notice and cite it (APA 7th) if your task requires published sources.
